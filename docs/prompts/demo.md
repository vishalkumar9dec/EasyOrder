# Top use cases:
1. Group Ordering: Order 2 Chicken Biryani, 1 Mixed Veg, 3 Tandoori roti from best restaurant under 2000 INR. Host and Guests can see, verify, modify the order on the UI on the shared link.
2. Ordering from a specific restaurant: "Order paneer tikka and chicken 65 from MotiMahal restaurant and if the budget exceeds INR 800, let me know before placing the order".
3. Scheduling order: "Order Egg Biryani for me from BiryaniByKilo and it should reach by 8PM" (Before 8PM mean 30 mins time window, anytime between 7:30 to 8:00 PM). 

# Script 
1. Two side by side running for Party discussion going on WhatsApp group, Once the menu is decided:
Left side: Host manually launches Swiggy and places multiple orders 1 by 1 while keeping a check on the budget manually. Tense, time taking, frustrating process sometimes. Guests don't know which restaurants the order is placed.
Right side: Host simply shares the menu to EasyOrder in natural language and EasyOrder automatically takes care of the multiple orders, budget. Host does not have to scroll endless menus, manually keep track of the budget and lives tension, frustration free life.
2. Ordering from a specific restaurant
Left side: User opens Swiggy, finds the restaurant, scrolls the menu, selects the dish, verifies the order, places the order.
Right side: User tells EasyOrder in natural laungage, verifies, places the order. EasyOrder literally takes out 4 steps from the picture. Moreover, in the future version, order can be placed by EasyOrder by itself after the user verifies the order.
